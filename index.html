THE PROBLEMS
I LIKE ARE
RARELY CLEAN.

  • No signal. Work must continue offline.
  • Bad lighting. The real world isn't perfect.
  • Legacy code. Systems older than the team.
  • Millions of records. Data at scale, every day.
  • Real-world sensors. Unreliable. Noisy. Unpredictable.
  • AI that has to act. Not just chat, but take action.

The interesting problems are rarely the tidy ones.

  1. 01 Sometimes the network disappears halfway through a job.
  2. 02 Sometimes thousands of records have to move between local and remote.
  3. 03 Sometimes a phone has to understand physical space.
  4. 04 Sometimes an assistant has to complete the work, not just describe it.
  5. 05 Sometimes sensors disagree, and the software has to decide what to trust.
  6. 06 Sometimes the hardest part is changing software people already depend on.

Behind the systems

I'm Nikunj Prajapati.

Lead iOS Software Engineer & iOS Architect

I build Apple-platform systems where mobile, data, sensors and intelligence meet the real world, usually on problems that turn out harder than the technology first suggests.

The question used to be "does this feature work?" These days it is closer to:

  • Behaves with no connection?
  • Holds up under load?
  • Works on a three-year-old device?
  • Can the architecture still change?
